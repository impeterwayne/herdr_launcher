# OpenCode Command: /openspec-explore

Explore the codebase and existing specifications to gather context and identify requirements.


# OpenSpec Explore Skill

Use this skill when you need to research existing architecture, review current specifications in `openspec/specs/`, and understand the scope of a feature or bug fix.

## Workflow
1. Inspect `openspec/project.md` and `openspec/specs/`.
2. Search codebase for relevant components and symbols.
3. Identify affected areas, dependencies, and potential constraints.
4. Output a summary of findings to prepare for proposing changes.
